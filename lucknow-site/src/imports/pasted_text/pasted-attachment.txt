You are a senior product designer, UX architect, UI designer and frontend design-system specialist.

Design the official destination/place page for D360 (Darshan360), a travel and tourism discovery platform by EduFutura Technologies Pvt Ltd.

IMPORTANT PRODUCT CONCEPT

D360 is not a generic hotel-booking website.

D360 helps users discover:
- unexplored places
- underrated destinations
- heritage locations
- historical places
- cultural destinations
- natural destinations
- local experiences
- hidden gems
- food and local culture
- nearby destinations
- complete trip possibilities

The same destination-page UI will eventually be reused for hundreds or thousands of destinations.

Examples:
Agra
Gwalior
Kanpur
Mumbai
Delhi

Darjeeling
Puducherry
small towns
villages
forts

natural attractions

Therefore:

DO NOT design Agra as a one-off page.

Design a UNIVERSAL D360 DESTINATION PAGE SYSTEM where the UI remains identical and only the destination data changes.

Use AGRA, UTTAR PRADESH as the example destination.
